MUF (measured): at least the highest band the beacon was heard on; an upper bound when the next band up was tried twice or more and never heard (a beacon that is off the air or fading also looks closed). SSB chance (estimate, rule of thumb): the 100 W dash S/N in 30 Hz minus 19 dB = S/N in a 2.4 kHz SSB bandwidth; about +6 dB is a workable contact (50 %), times the share of tries it was heard. Assumes you run 100 W into an antenna like the beacon's on a reciprocal path; receiver AGC makes it err low.
